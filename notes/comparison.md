# Method-level comparison matrix

Input contracts are not interchangeable. These are targeted reading notes, not reproduced scores. Each title links to a fuller audit card.

| Paper | Composition axes | Inputs checked | Mechanism | Evaluation checked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [motion-annotations](papers.md#motion-annotations) | temporal | Positive, negative and unconstrained timeline labels; optional pose, position and orientation constraints. | Coarse-to-fine dynamic programming searches database motion blocks; SVM-assisted annotation expands a small labeled subset. | Interactive examples include trip, run and jump constraints and combined walking/carrying semantics. |
| [stmc](papers.md#stmc) | spatial, temporal | Multi-track text timeline with timing and body-part assignments, provided by user or language model. | Preprocesses prompts into per-body-part timelines; denoises local crops and combines corresponding pose components repeatedly during sampling. | Temporal and simultaneous composition; assess under the paper’s timeline setting rather than single-prompt metrics alone. |
| [flowmdm](papers.md#flowmdm) | temporal | Non-overlapping text intervals and durations; can train on singly described clips. | Early denoising uses absolute positions for within-action structure; late denoising uses relative positions across boundaries. Pose-centric cross-attention associates each frame with its own condition. | BABEL and HumanML3D subsequence and transition quality, plus Peak Jerk and Area Under Jerk for discontinuities. |
| [priormdm](papers.md#priormdm) | temporal | Interval text prompts; two-person motion examples for ComMDM; prescribed joint or trajectory motion for fine-grained control. | A frozen short-motion diffusion prior is reused for overlapping prompted intervals; a second take refines transitions. Separate small adapters or fine-tuned controlled priors support other composition modes. | Off-the-shelf motion diffusion prior compared with task-specific trained models across sequential, parallel and controlled-generation tasks. |
| [fusion](papers.md#fusion) | semantic, spatial | Keypoint tracking supplies future joint constraints. HOI uses object motion and a GRABNet grasp assumed stable during motion. Self-interaction uses LLM-derived contacts/timesteps; object motion is not generated. | Unconditional SMPL-X diffusion prior jointly models body/hands; task losses backpropagate through denoising to optimize noise. Local hand transfer preserves body wrists and filters additional penetration. | HumanML3D tracking errors; MotionCritic body realism; fingertip/hand perception and self-interaction ablations. Standard text-motion FID/R-precision are not reported for its representation. |
| [frankenmotion](papers.md#frankenmotion) | semantic, spatial, temporal | Temporally aligned, potentially sparse sequence, action and seven-body-part text; FrankenStein extends existing mocap annotations using LLM reasoning. | CLIP action/part embeddings are PCA-reduced to 50 dimensions, expanded framewise and concatenated with noisy motion through an MLP; sequence and diffusion-step features are extra tokens. Unknown labels are zeroed; Beta-distributed random masking improves sparse-conditioning robustness. | UniMotion, STMC and DART are adapted and retrained on FrankenStein using the same pose representation. Nine separate TMR-style evaluators assess seven parts, actions and sequences using R-Precision/M2T; realism uses FID/Diversity. Evaluations repeat 20 times with 95% confidence intervals. Annotation audit: 50 sequences, three experts. |
| [demogen](papers.md#demogen) | semantic, spatial | Exp requires decomposed text; OSS/SC infer concepts without decomposed inference prompts. DeCompML splits HumanML3D captions using GPT-4.1; default decomposition uses two concepts. | Frozen-VAE motion latents are denoised with summed concept-conditioned predictions (latent-aware), or decomposed cross-attention (semantic-aware). Exp uses decomposed captions; OSS splits text embeddings with orthogonality; SC adds semantic consistency against decomposed embeddings. | HumanML3D-pretrained models are directly evaluated on MTT and DeCompML. Exp handles multiple-text composition; OSS/SC handle single-text multi-concept generation. DeCompML combines decomposed captions with HumanML3D test motions and uses its text-motion evaluator. MTT reports R-Precision, TMR scores, FID and transition distance. |
| [energymogen](papers.md#energymogen) | semantic, spatial | Multiple textual concepts, logical conjunction/negation; whole-motion latent representation rather than explicit per-frame body-part stitching. | Classifier-free diffusion predictions implement latent-aware energy composition. Cross-attention provides semantic-aware composition and adaptive text-embedding gradient updates; Synergistic Energy Fusion reconciles the two. Main inference uses five latent vectors and 50 diffusion steps. | HumanML3D/KIT-ML evaluate ordinary text generation. MTT compares combined single text against multiple-text composition using original constituent captions. Metrics follow STMC: R-Precision, TMR-Score, FID, transition distance. CompML contains 5,000 generated pairs from random triples of STMC captions, used with HumanML3D for augmentation experiments. |
| [sinc](papers.md#sinc) | data, semantic, spatial | Text specifying simultaneous actions and a body-part inventory. | GPT-3 identifies active body parts; body-part motion combinations create synthetic examples for text-to-motion training. | Compositional generation compared with baselines; improvement from synthetic part compositions is reported. |
| [cosmi](papers.md#cosmi) | data, object-contact, spatial | Text and object conditions; composed sources provide body-part/object relationships for training. | Transfer interacting body parts with their objects, use mirrored sources, semantic feasibility and geometric rejection; weight-shared object slots implement variable-count diffusion. | Benchmark includes unseen interaction combinations and unseen object; text alignment and contact metrics compare models and dataset transfer. |
| [duethoi](papers.md#duethoi) | object-contact | Language, canonical geometry and initial articulation. ArtPlanner’s test reference is predicted from its conditional prior; its training posterior sees GT articulation. This is hand/object generation, without locomotion. | ContactVAE samples contact intent; conditional-prior ArtPlanner predicts scalar articulation, ManiVAE compresses hands, and DualFormer couples token/time streams. ProxiRefine corrects hands while fixing generated object motion. | ARCTIC articulated/rigid and one/two-hand modes; GRAB rigid modes; adapted Text2HOI, LatentHOI and MDM; contact, hand/object errors, diversity and relative consistency; component/refinement ablations. |
| [sk-hoi](papers.md#sk-hoi) | object-contact | Text, object BPS geometry and initial human/object state; alternatives supply sparse object waypoints or full future object trajectories. Main generated object paths are predictions, not automatically GT. | Three transformer/flow-matching stages generate rigid-component keypoints, contact-distance fields and body markers; inference optimizes body diffusion noise against contact and object-SDF penetration losses. | ParaHome, HIMO, ARCTIC and OMOMO; FID, text alignment, foot sliding, interaction geometry/contact and object jerk; representation/contact ablations. |
| [dechoi](papers.md#dechoi) | object-contact | Text, object BPS geometry, first human/object poses and final object position. Intermediate waypoints are not prescribed. Action-stage paths are TG predictions; Appendix E.3 separately gives all-frame GT paths as an oracle. | Trajectory diffusion generates human/object paths; action diffusion then conditions densely on these predicted positions. Distal-joint adversarial training and reconstruction guidance target contact and grounded feet. | FullBodyManipulation and unseen same-category 3D-FUTURE geometry; condition/contact/motion/GT errors, ablations, perception study and DynaPlan dynamic planning. |
| [hoifhli](papers.md#hoifhli) | object-contact, scene-state, temporal | Full system: instruction, scene/assets and initial states. Low-level CoarseNet additionally requires final object pose and 2D waypoints every 30 frames; planner-generated conditions must be distinguished from GT-extracted paths. | LLM scene planning and A* navigation feed CoarseNet; predicted contact phases drive grasp generation, wrist-relative RefineNet and FingerNet. RL separately tracks the generated reference. | FullBodyManipulation low-level comparisons include CHOIS+GRIP; waypoint, foot, hand/object contact, intersection and pose metrics plus perception study; physics tracker is evaluated separately. |
| [primhoi](papers.md#primhoi) | object-contact, scene-state, temporal | Task description, scene/object geometry and symbolic domain knowledge; planned subgoals and object/contact trajectories are internal generated conditions, not a claim of text+initial-state-only direct diffusion. | Author method overview: LLM compiles a task into PDDL-HOI; symbolic planning yields subgoal graphs. Primitive contact factors generate key poses; object paths precede contact-guided intermediate human motion and constraint post-optimization. | Official page demonstrates four primitive contact modes, varied shapes/placements and three extended plans. Detailed quantitative evaluation was not re-audited because CVF PDF fetch returned 403. |
| [himo](papers.md#himo) | object-contact, spatial, temporal | Whole or segmented text prompts, object geometry and initial states; temporal segments supply fine control. | Dual human/object diffusion branches exchange information through mutual interaction; segmented generation conditions later intervals on preceding motion. | HIMO generation, temporal composition and unseen geometry experiments; history-context and model-component ablations. |
| [chois](papers.md#chois) | object-contact | Language, object BPS geometry, initial human/object state, 2D object positions every 30 frames and final 3D object position. | Transformer denoises synchronized human poses, object poses and hand-contact labels; object geometry loss improves waypoint adherence and sampling guidance constrains contacts. | OMOMO manipulation benchmark, unseen-object analysis, motion/contact/waypoint metrics, guidance ablations and scene-planner demonstrations. |
| [procigen](papers.md#procigen) | object-contact | Data synthesis requires captured seed interaction poses and same-category replacement geometry. HDM takes one RGB image without an object template; output is static shape/interaction reconstruction. | Category-specific autoencoder correspondences transfer contacts to replacement objects; joint pose optimization preserves contact and reduces collisions, followed by textured rendering. HDM reconstructs joint then separate human/object point clouds. | BEHAVE/InterCap reconstruction F-score at 1 cm, data/model ablations, cross-dataset and unseen-shape/category evaluation. |
| [world2hosi](papers.md#world2hosi) | object-contact, scene-state, temporal | Language, reconstructed scene/background and object assets, expert-specific initial/goal/path conditions produced by the harness. | Structured MotionIR and state updates compile expert conditions; incoming-prefix translation/SO(3) offsets decay with quintic smoothstep. Bounded residual RL tracks references and objects move through simulated contacts. | Shared tasks versus external systems, user study, fixed-segment boundary ablation, reference-tracking errors and state-dependent multi-stage demonstrations. |
| [dimos](papers.md#dimos) | scene-state | Scene geometry, navigation goals/path waypoints and fine-grained target body markers; interaction goals can be generated by COINS. | Policies select latent actions of generative motion primitives; navigation uses walkability features and waypoints, interaction uses SDF proximity and marker goals. | Locomotion and fine-grained interaction benchmarks, varied object geometry/orientation and body starts, interaction-sequence demonstrations and feature/reward ablations. |
| [intermask](papers.md#intermask) | multi-person | Main task: text with both actors initially masked. Reaction: full reference actor motion stays unmasked, with optional text; this is future actor-conditioned completion, not a demonstrated causal online policy. | Shared 2D VQ-VAE preserves spatial/temporal tokens; collaborative transformer combines self, spatiotemporal and cross-person attention. Iterative confidence remasking generates both actors jointly. | InterHuman/InterX FID, R-precision, MMDist, diversity/multimodality; attention/masking/tokenizer ablations, reaction tests and user study. |
| [intergen](papers.md#intergen) | multi-person | Natural-language description; downstream editing/completion variants supply additional motion conditions. | Shared denoisers, mutual attention, world-frame joint representation and spatial-relation regularization with damping preserve interpersonal coordination. | InterHuman text-to-interaction benchmark, generation quality/diversity and text alignment comparisons, representation/regularization ablations and editing applications. |
| [remos](papers.md#remos) | multi-person | Complete actor body/hand motion over the target interval is supplied. Reactor motion is generated; this is future actor-conditioned completion rather than two-person generation from text or causal online reaction. | Cascaded diffusion first generates reactive body joints using joint spatiotemporal cross-attention, then hands with proximity-derived interaction masks; reaction/kinematic losses and inference guidance improve alignment. | ReMoCap Lindy Hop/Ninjutsu, 2C and ExPI; MPJPE/velocity error, body/hand FID, diversity, ablations and user study; appendix adds InterHuman comparisons. |
| [parahome](papers.md#parahome) | data | Text baseline generates human motion. Object-guided baseline supplies object motion over the full target window plus spatial relations/initial state; it does not predict object motion from text+initial state. | New capture system aligns multicamera object tracking with IMU body/glove motion and post-processing. Baselines study text-conditioned body/hand synthesis and transformer object-state-conditioned body synthesis. | Capture/calibration and post-processing checks; ParaHome-only/HumanML3D-only/mixed text-motion metrics; object-guided positional errors and foot skating; latent interpolation demonstrations. |
| [humanml3d](papers.md#humanml3d) | data | Natural-language description; sampled or supplied length. | Samples duration from text, then generates motion snippets with a temporal VAE. | HumanML3D and KIT-ML; FID, diversity, multimodality, R-precision and text-motion distance. R-precision ranks the paired caption against 31 distractors. |
| [phc](papers.md#phc) | physical-skill | Reference pose sequence and simulated humanoid state. | Progressive multiplicative control allocates capacity for difficult motions and recovery. | Large-scale mocap imitation, noisy video/language references and fail-state recovery timing; simulation avatar evidence. |
| [weave](papers.md#weave) | embodiment, object-contact, physical-skill, scene-state, temporal | Robot/object state, geometric and reference interaction information. | Constructs executable interaction references, then tracks them with coordinated body and finger control. | Nine-object simulation suite, trained and held-out sequences plus architecture/optimizer ablations. Approximately 9,000 rollouts/23 hours are simulated execution. |
| [moco](papers.md#moco) | cross-modal | Concurrent speech audio, action text and target trajectory; no paired multimodal training set required by the author description. | Four modality/component denoisers use body masks; a timeline can reassign parts and an LLM can plan intervals. | Author page: 1,000 concurrent-control clips built from 40 text descriptions and 694 speech clips; evaluates text and speech fidelity. |
